ArchiSurance sample model. The file samples/archisurance/Archisurance.archimate is copied unchanged from the test data of Archi, the open source ArchiMate modelling tool (https://github.com/archimatetool/archi). It is Copyright (c) 2013-2026 Phillip Beauvoir, Jean-Baptiste Sarrodie, The Open Group, and is used under the MIT licence, reproduced in full in samples/archisurance/LICENSE-Archi.txt. ArchiSurance is a fictional insurance company originally created by The Open Group to illustrate the ArchiMate language.

The ArchiSurance example atlas (model files, dashboard, Excel repository and atlas file) is derived from that model by Episteme Partners. Everything not in the Archi model, including vendors, products, costs, contracts, service levels, ratings, roles, journeys, regulation mappings, the change portfolio and the consolidation programme, is invented for illustration. It does not describe any real organisation and is not part of The Open Group's ArchiSurance case study.

Industry standards and frameworks

The industry packs are written in Episteme Partners' own words. Where a pack refers to an industry standard, it records only the name of the closest category in the std_ref field, so that users can find the matching part of the standard themselves. The packs do not reproduce the definitions, descriptions, structures, models or other content of these standards. To use a standard itself, obtain it from its owner and follow the owner's terms.

  • BIAN. The BIAN Service Landscape and its service domains are published by the Banking Industry Architecture Network (https://bian.org). The banking pack refers to BIAN service domain names only.
  • ACORD. The ACORD Framework is licensed by ACORD to its members (https://www.acord.org). The insurance pack does not use ACORD content. "ACORD-style" describes the pack's own grouping of insurance domains, which is similar in scope; it is not the ACORD Framework.
  • APQC. The Process Classification Framework (PCF) is published by APQC (https://www.apqc.org). The core pack refers to PCF category names only.
  • ArchiMate and TOGAF. The ArchiMate modelling language and the TOGAF standard are published by The Open Group (https://www.opengroup.org). EA Atlas reads and writes ArchiMate models through Archi files and the Open Group exchange format; it does not reproduce the specifications.

Regulations

The regulation libraries summarise legislation and guidance such as the EU Digital Operational Resilience Act (DORA), the Solvency II review, NIS2, the GDPR, the EU AI Act and the proposed EU Payment Services Regulation. The summaries are paraphrased by Episteme Partners from the public sources cited in each pack, including EUR-Lex (https://eur-lex.europa.eu, (c) European Union, reused under the Commission's reuse policy). Only the texts published in the Official Journal of the European Union are authentic. Dates marked "estimate" or "to confirm" must be checked against the final texts.
