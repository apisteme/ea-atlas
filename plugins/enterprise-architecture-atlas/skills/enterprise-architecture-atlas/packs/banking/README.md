# Banking pack

Built from a retail and SME bank reference implementation, with every organisation-specific and country-specific fact removed.

- Capability model: 12 L1, 75 L2, 290 L3 in the pack's own words; `std_ref` names the closest BIAN service domain. BIAN content is published under BIAN's own terms; check them before republishing anything beyond names.
- Criticality concept: critical or important function (DORA). Third-party concept: material outsourcing (EBA outsourcing guidelines) and DORA Article 30 contract terms.
- Templates: 75 roles (business, technology, data, security), 4 value streams with 12 journeys and 65 steps, 53 processes.
- Regulations: DORA (internal remediation target), EU PSR (20 requirements from public summaries of the April 2026 compromise text; dates are estimates), EU AI Act (credit scoring is high-risk).
- Sizing guide for a retail bank: 60 to 70 applications, about 50 suppliers, about 70 interfaces, 10 to 15 epics with about 40 features.
