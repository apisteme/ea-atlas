# Insurance pack

- Capability model: 11 L1, 61 L2, 143 L3 in the pack's own words; `std_ref` names an insurance domain in the style of the ACORD Framework. The ACORD Framework is licensed to ACORD members, so the pack records domain names only.
- Criticality concept: critical or important function (DORA; Solvency II outsourcing). Third-party concept: critical outsourcing and DORA Article 30 terms.
- Templates: 54 roles, 3 value streams with 5 journeys (take out insurance, handle a claim, change a policy, renew, complaint), 39 processes. Taken from the ArchiSurance example with its names made generic.
- Regulations: DORA (internal remediation target), Solvency II review (Directive (EU) 2025/2, applies from 30 January 2027), EU AI Act (life and health pricing is high-risk; stand-alone high-risk obligations moved to 2 December 2027 under the 2026 provisional agreement, to confirm).
- Sizing guide for a mid-size insurer: 30 to 60 applications, 20 to 40 suppliers, 40 to 80 interfaces.
